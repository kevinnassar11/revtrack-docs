Delete your RevTrack account and data

How to permanently remove your account and everything associated with it.

You can ask us to delete your RevTrack account and all data tied to it at any time. You do not need to give a reason.

How to request deletion

  1. Email arevtrack@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to RevTrack.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my RevTrack account.
  3. We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days.

We ask you to email from your sign-in address so we can verify the request is genuinely yours. If you no longer have access to that address, contact us anyway and we will find another way to verify you.

What gets deleted

When you request deletion, we permanently remove:

DataWhat it is
Your account recordYour name, email address, and account identifier.
Your leaderboard entryYour handle, car tag, distance, ride count and top speed. You disappear from every other driver's leaderboard.
Your follow relationshipsEveryone you follow, and everyone following you.
Your track statisticsEvery per-track best time and top speed recorded under your account.
Your device tokensThe push-notification tokens registered to your devices. You stop receiving notifications.
Your uploaded photosAny vehicle photos held on our side, including any submitted for 3D reconstruction.

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its history once the request has been completed, so please be sure before you ask.

Data stored only on your phone

Your recorded rides, routes, garage details, fuel and maintenance entries are stored locally on your device, not on our servers. They are removed when you uninstall RevTrack or clear the app's storage from Android Settings. Deleting your account does not by itself erase them from your phone — uninstall the app as well if you want everything gone.

Deleting some data but keeping your account

If you would rather not delete the whole account:

  • Stop sharing location: revoke the location permission in Android Settings. Ride recording stops working, but the rest of the app continues.
  • Stop push notifications: revoke the notification permission in Android Settings. Your device token becomes inactive.
  • Clear your local history: use Reset ride log on the Profile screen to wipe your recorded rides from the device.
  • Leave the leaderboard: email us and ask for your leaderboard entry and track stats to be removed without deleting your account.

What we keep, and for how long

After deletion we retain nothing that identifies you. We may keep minimal, non-identifying records (for example, that a deletion request was completed on a given date) where we need them to demonstrate compliance with data protection obligations. These cannot be linked back to you.
